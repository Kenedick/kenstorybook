THE DRAGON GAMES Episode 1: The Girl Chosen by the Dragon **Genre:** Fantasy • Adventure • Action • Mystery ### Chapter 1: The Selection For eighteen years, Aria Vale had lived in a kingdom where dragons were feared, powerful, and nearly impossible to control. Every year, the royal family held the Dragon Games, a dangerous competition where selected young people faced challenges to prove their courage, intelligence, and strength. The winner would receive a royal title, a fortune, and the honor of becoming a dragon rider. But there was one rule everyone knew. **No one could force a dragon to choose them.** Aria had never planned to participate. She lived in a small village with her younger brother, Finn, and worked hard to keep their family alive after their parents disappeared years ago. Her life changed on the morning the royal guards arrived. “By order of the king,” the captain announced, unrolling a golden scroll, “the following citizens have been selected for this year's Dragon Games.” The villagers gathered in the town square. Names were called one by one. Then the captain spoke a name that made Aria's blood run cold. “Aria Vale.” Everyone turned toward her. “What? No!” Aria stepped forward. “There must be a mistake!” The captain showed her the royal seal. “There are no mistakes in the selection.” Finn grabbed her hand. “You can't go! The Games are dangerous!” Aria tried to remain calm, but she knew the truth. Refusing a royal summons could bring punishment upon her entire family. She knelt in front of her brother. “Listen to me, Finn. I'll come back. I promise.” But deep inside, she wasn't sure she could keep that promise. ### Chapter 2: The Dragon Academy Three days later, Aria arrived at the royal capital. The Dragon Academy stood on top of a mountain, surrounded by enormous stone walls and towers. Dragons circled above the buildings, their wings casting enormous shadows across the courtyard. Aria had never seen so many dragons in her life. Some had silver scales that glittered in the sunlight. Others had dark wings, golden horns, or fiery eyes. A young man wearing a black uniform approached her. “First time seeing a dragon up close?” he asked. Aria glanced at him. “Is it that obvious?” “You look like you're about to run away.” “Maybe I am.” He laughed. “My name is Kael Draven.” Aria recognized the surname immediately. The Draven family was one of the most powerful noble families in the kingdom. Its members had been famous dragon riders for generations. “Aria Vale,” she replied. Kael's smile faded slightly. “Vale? Are you related to the missing dragon rider, Elena Vale?” Aria stiffened. “That's my mother.” For a moment, Kael said nothing. Then he looked toward the highest tower of the academy. “You should be careful here,” he warned. “Your mother's name is not one people like to hear.” Before Aria could ask why, a bell rang across the courtyard. The contestants were being summoned. ### Chapter 3: The First Trial Hundreds of contestants gathered inside a massive arena surrounded by high stone walls. At the center stood a platform where the headmaster waited. “Welcome to the Dragon Games,” he announced. “Your first trial is simple. Enter the dragon sanctuary and allow a dragon to approach you. If no dragon chooses you before sunset, you will be eliminated.” The gates opened. The contestants entered a vast valley filled with dragons resting among the rocks. Some young people immediately approached the nearest dragons, offering food and speaking gently. Others stood frozen with fear. Aria remained near the entrance. She knew nothing about dragon riding. She had no special training, no powerful family, and no experience fighting. Her only advantage was that she refused to give up. As she walked deeper into the sanctuary, she heard a strange sound. A low growl. She turned. Behind a cluster of black rocks stood an enormous dragon with midnight-colored scales. One of its wings appeared injured, and a metal chain was wrapped around its front leg. Unlike the other dragons, this one had no saddle or rider. A nearby contestant noticed Aria staring. “Stay away from that beast!” he shouted. “That's Shadowfang. It attacked three handlers!” Aria looked at the dragon's injured wing. “It looks hurt.” “Are you insane? It'll kill you!” But Aria continued forward. She slowly approached the dragon, keeping her hands visible. “I'm not going to hurt you,” she whispered. The dragon lifted its enormous head. Its golden eyes locked onto hers. For a moment, the entire sanctuary seemed to fall silent. Aria stopped several steps away. “I know you're afraid,” she said softly. “I'm afraid, too.” The dragon lowered its head. Then, to everyone's astonishment, it moved closer. A brilliant silver light burst from the ground beneath Aria's feet. The contestants screamed as the dragon spread its wings. A symbol appeared on Aria's wrist: a silver flame surrounded by the shape of a dragon. The headmaster stared in disbelief. “No. That mark hasn't appeared in a hundred years.” Kael watched from the other side of the sanctuary, his expression unreadable. “It's impossible,” he whispered. Shadowfang lowered its enormous head until its forehead gently touched Aria's. A voice suddenly echoed inside her mind. *Finally, Elena's daughter.* Aria's breath caught. The dragon had spoken directly to her. And it knew her mother. ### Chapter 4: A Secret from the Past That evening, Aria was taken to the headmaster's office. The headmaster paced beside a tall window while Shadowfang's symbol glowed faintly on her wrist. “Tell me exactly what happened in the sanctuary,” he demanded. “I approached the dragon. It looked at me, and then the mark appeared.” “Did the dragon communicate with you?” Aria hesitated. She had heard the voice clearly, but she didn't know whether she could trust the academy. “No,” she lied. The headmaster studied her for a long moment. “Your mother was once the most promising dragon rider in this kingdom. She disappeared after an incident involving a forbidden dragon.” Aria's heart began pounding. “What happened to her?” “That information is classified.” “She was my mother! I deserve to know!” The headmaster's expression hardened. “You deserve to survive the Games first.” He dismissed her without another explanation. When Aria returned to her room, she found an envelope placed beneath her door. Inside was a small photograph of her mother standing beside a black dragon. On the back, someone had written a message. *If Shadowfang has chosen you, the king will never let you leave the academy.* Beneath the message was a second line. *Your mother did not disappear. She was betrayed.* Aria stared at the words, her hands trembling. A knock sounded at her door. She quickly hid the photograph. “Who is it?” “Kael,” came the reply. “We need to talk.” Aria opened the door slightly. Kael looked serious. “I know what happened today,” he said. “And I know why the headmaster is afraid of you.” “Then tell me the truth.” He glanced down the hallway to make sure they were alone. “Shadowfang isn't an ordinary dragon. It's the last dragon of an ancient bloodline, and your mother was the only person who ever rode it.” Aria's world seemed to stop. Kael stepped closer and lowered his voice. “If the king discovers what that mark means, the Dragon Games will become the least of your problems.” Suddenly, an alarm rang throughout the academy. Red lights flashed along the walls. A guard's voice echoed through the corridors. “Attention! Shadowfang has escaped the sanctuary!” Aria turned toward the window. Outside, the enormous black dragon was flying toward the royal palace. Then the voice returned inside her mind. *Aria, come to the palace before midnight.* She looked at Kael. “What happens if I don't?” His face turned pale. “Then the king will find Shadowfang first.” Far beyond the academy walls, the royal palace glowed beneath the moonlight. And in its highest tower, the king opened an ancient book bearing Aria's family name. “She's alive,” he murmured. He closed the book. “Bring me Elena Vale's daughter.” TO BE CONTINUED… End of Episode 1
