Episode 1: The Fallen Heiress and the Cold Commander Genre: Sci-Fi • Romance • Arranged Marriage • Revenge • Drama Chapter 1: The Day She Lost Everything The day Elena Starling lost her position as the youngest fleet commander in the Galactic Empire, the entire capital celebrated. For years, Elena had defended the empire's outer colonies, defeated invading forces, and earned the respect of thousands of soldiers. But after a political conspiracy accused her of betraying the imperial fleet, everything she had built collapsed overnight. Her military rank was revoked. Her family withdrew their support. And the man she had trusted most testified against her. Standing inside the imperial court, Elena listened as the judge announced her sentence. “Commander Elena Starling, you are hereby removed from your position and stripped of your military privileges.” Elena kept her head high. “Is that all?” The judge frowned. “You have nothing to say in your defense?” “I already presented the evidence. If this court refuses to examine it, nothing I say will change your decision.” Whispers spread throughout the chamber. Elena turned and walked away, refusing to give her enemies the satisfaction of seeing her break. Outside the courthouse, reporters crowded around her. “Commander Starling! Did you really sell military secrets?” “Is it true that your family abandoned you?” “Do you regret serving the empire?” Elena ignored every question. She had lost her rank, her reputation, and nearly everyone she trusted. But she still had one thing her enemies had underestimated. Her mind. And she intended to use it to discover who had framed her.
