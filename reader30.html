Chapter 1 introduces Sunset Towers, a mysterious luxury apartment building along Lake Michigan that, despite its name, faces east into the sunrise and features no actual towers. A slick salesman operating under the pseudonym "Barney Northrup" rents out all the spaces—including residential units, offices, and restaurants—to a handpicked group of tenants by making each feel as though they were receiving the very last available apartment. Though Northrup successfully leases every unit in a single day, the narrator reveals that he made a critical error: one of the tenants selected was invited by mistake, and among the seemingly quiet residents are a bookie, a burglar, a bomber, and an unsuspecting target.
