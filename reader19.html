THE SIX MNEMOSYNE Episode 1: The Girl Who Remembered Everything Genre: Fantasy • Mystery • Supernatural • Thriller Chapter 1: The Forgotten Memory Luna Everhart had always been different. Since childhood, she had experienced strange dreams about places she had never visited and people she had never met. Sometimes, she could remember conversations that nobody else seemed to recall. Her grandmother used to tell her one thing whenever she spoke about these experiences. “Some memories are gifts, Luna. Others are warnings.” Luna never understood what those words meant—until the night her grandmother disappeared. Seven years later, Luna was a university student trying to live an ordinary life. She had almost forgotten the strange stories from her childhood. Then, one rainy evening, she received an envelope with no return address. Inside was an old photograph showing six teenagers standing in front of a ruined temple. On the back, someone had written: THE SIX MNEMOSYNE MUST NEVER REMEMBER. Luna turned the photograph over again. Her heart stopped. One of the six people in the picture was her grandmother. And standing beside her was a girl who looked exactly like Luna.
