DILDARA Episode 1: The Girl Behind the Mystery The rain poured heavily as a young woman ran through the dark streets, clutching a small envelope against her chest. “Please… I need to find him,” she whispered. Her name was Dildara. To everyone who knew her, she was just an ordinary girl with a quiet personality and a mysterious past. But behind her gentle smile was a secret that could change everything. **Three months earlier…** “Dildara! You're going to be late on your first day!” I heard my best friend shouting outside my bedroom. “I'm coming!” I replied, quickly fixing my hair. Today was my first day at Silvercrest University. I had promised myself that this would be a fresh start. No more running away from my past. No more secrets controlling my life. At least, that was what I believed. When I arrived at the university, everyone seemed to know one another. Students laughed together while others talked about their summer vacations. I took a deep breath and walked toward the main building. Suddenly, someone bumped into me. My books fell to the floor. “I'm sorry!” I said, bending down to collect them. A hand reached for the last book before I could grab it. “Be careful next time.” I looked up. Standing in front of me was a handsome young man with dark eyes and a serious expression. “Maybe you should watch where you're going, too,” I answered. He raised an eyebrow, apparently surprised by my response. “Interesting.” He handed me my book and walked away without another word. “Arrogant,” I muttered. I thought that would be the end of our encounter. I was wrong. During our first class, the professor announced a group project. “Dildara, you'll be working with Adrian Blackwell.” My eyes widened. The same guy from the hallway. Adrian glanced at me from the other side of the room. “Looks like we're meeting again.” I crossed my arms. “Unfortunately.” He smiled slightly. “I think this semester will be interesting.” I ignored him and looked out the window. Then I noticed something hanging from his bag. A silver necklace. My heart suddenly stopped. I knew that necklace. It was identical to the one my mother had given me before she disappeared years ago. How could Adrian have it? Before I could ask, he noticed me staring. “Do you recognize this?” he asked, holding the necklace. I hesitated. “No,” I lied. His expression turned serious. “That's strange,” he whispered. “Because someone told me that a girl named Dildara would know the truth about it.” My blood ran cold. How did he know my name? And what did he know about my mother? For the first time in years, I felt that my past was finally catching up with me. And Adrian Blackwell might be the key to everything.
