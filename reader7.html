BRIDE BY MISTAKE Episode 1: The Wrong Groom “WHAT DO YOU MEAN I'M GETTING MARRIED TODAY?!” My voice echoed throughout the hotel room. I stared at the white wedding dress hanging beside the window, then at the woman standing in front of me. My best friend, Sophia, looked like she wanted to disappear. “Okay, before you get mad, let me explain—” “EXPLAIN WHAT?! WHY AM I WEARING A WEDDING DRESS?!” “Because your sister asked me to help you get ready!” I blinked. “My sister?” Sophia nodded slowly. That was when I remembered everything. My twin sister, Isabelle, was supposed to get married today. But last night, she called me crying, saying she couldn't go through with the wedding. I thought she was joking. Apparently, she wasn't. And somehow, I had ended up in her wedding dress. “I'm leaving,” I declared, grabbing my bag. Sophia immediately blocked the door. “You can't! The guests are already here!” “Then tell them the bride ran away!” “And let your family deal with the scandal?” I stopped. My family had spent months preparing for this wedding. The groom came from one of the wealthiest families in the country, and canceling everything at the last minute would cause chaos. Still, I wasn't about to marry a stranger. “What's his name?” I asked. Sophia hesitated. “Alexander Sterling.” I frowned. Wait. Alexander Sterling? The billionaire CEO known for his cold personality and his refusal to appear in public unless absolutely necessary? “Oh, absolutely not.” Before I could escape, someone knocked on the door. “Miss, the ceremony is about to begin.” My stomach dropped. There was no time left. --- ### The Wedding I walked down the aisle with trembling hands, trying to keep my face calm. Everyone smiled at me. Nobody knew I was the wrong bride. At the altar stood a tall man in a black suit. His expression was unreadable, and his eyes were sharp enough to make me nervous. Alexander Sterling. He looked at me as I approached. For a moment, I thought he had recognized me. But he said nothing. The ceremony began. When the officiant asked whether I accepted Alexander as my husband, I froze. This was insane. I didn't know this man. I didn't love him. I wasn't even supposed to be here! But before I could speak, Alexander leaned closer and whispered: “Smile. Everyone is watching.” I glared at him. “Do you even know who I am?” I whispered back. His expression didn't change. “I'm beginning to wonder.” My heart nearly stopped. He knew. Or at least, he suspected something was wrong. The ceremony continued, and before I could find a way out, the officiant announced us as husband and wife. Everyone applauded. I stood beside my new husband, smiling like the happiest bride in the world. Inside, I was planning my escape. --- ### The Billionaire's Warning That evening, I stood inside Alexander's luxurious penthouse, still wearing my wedding dress. He loosened his tie and looked directly at me. “Now,” he said calmly, “would you like to explain why my bride suddenly has a different face?” I swallowed. “It's a long story.” “Good. We have all night.” I took a deep breath. “My name is Amelia Sterling—” He raised an eyebrow. “Really?” I sighed. “Fine! My name is Amelia Carter. I'm Isabelle's twin sister. She ran away, and I was forced to take her place!” Silence. Alexander stared at me for several seconds. Then, unexpectedly, he laughed. It was brief, but it was real. “You married me by mistake,” he said. “I didn't want to marry you at all!” “Interesting.” He walked toward the window, looking out over the city. “Do you know what happens if we announce that this marriage was a mistake?” “My family will be embarrassed?” “Your family. My company. Both families' reputations.” I crossed my arms. “So, what do you want me to do?” He turned to face me. “Stay married to me. At least until we figure out how to resolve this.” My mouth fell open. “You're joking.” “I never joke about business.” “This is a marriage, not a business deal!” “Tonight, it's both.” I stared at him, unable to believe what I was hearing. Then he held out a document. “A temporary marriage agreement. Read it before you decide anything.” I looked at the papers, then at the man who was now technically my husband. This was supposed to be my sister's wedding. Instead, I had accidentally married a billionaire. And judging by Alexander Sterling's expression, my life was about to become much more complicated. Just as I reached for the document, his phone rang. He glanced at the screen, and his expression suddenly changed. “Where is she?” he demanded. A pause. Then he said something that made my blood run cold. “Find Isabelle. Now.” He ended the call and looked at me. “Amelia, there's something you need to know about your sister.” My heart pounded. “What is it?” Alexander's eyes met mine. “Your sister didn't run away because she was afraid of getting married.” He stepped closer. “She disappeared because someone threatened her.” **To be continued…** 💍 End of Episode 1
