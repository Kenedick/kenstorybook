FAKING PERFECTION Episode 1: The Perfect Girl Everyone at Westbridge University thought I had the perfect life. Perfect grades. Perfect appearance. Perfect friends. A family that everyone admired. But nobody knew that behind every smile was a girl who was slowly getting tired of pretending. And that girl was me. My name is Isabelle Morgan, and I have a secret. I'm not perfect. "Isabelle! Hurry up! The car is waiting!" Mom shouted from downstairs. I looked at myself in the mirror one last time, fixing my uniform and forcing a smile. There. Perfect. I grabbed my bag and went downstairs. "Good morning, sweetheart," Mom said, looking at me proudly. "Remember, you're representing our family. Don't disappoint us." I nodded. "Of course, Mom." Those three words had become my favorite lie. *Of course, Mom.* At school, everyone greeted me with smiles. "Good morning, Isabelle!" "Your presentation yesterday was amazing!" "How do you always get the highest score?" I smiled and thanked them, even though I barely slept the night before because I was studying. They thought I had everything under control. They didn't know how many times I wanted to scream that I was tired. During lunch, my best friend, Chloe, sat beside me. "Girl, are you okay?" she asked. "Of course! Why?" "Because you've been smiling at your food for five minutes." I laughed nervously. "I'm just thinking." "About what?" "Nothing important." Chloe looked at me suspiciously but didn't push further. After lunch, I went to the library to finish an assignment. I was so focused on my work that I didn't notice someone sitting across from me. "Do you ever stop working?" I looked up. A boy with messy black hair and a playful smile was staring at me. "Excuse me?" "You heard me. I've seen you around campus. You're always studying, always smiling, always acting like everything is perfect." I frowned. "And who are you to judge me?" He shrugged. "Someone who notices things." "Well, stop noticing me." He laughed quietly. "I'm Daniel Reyes, by the way." "I didn't ask." "Nice to meet you too, Isabelle Morgan." My eyes widened. "How do you know my name?" He pointed at my notebook, where my name was written on the cover. "Oh. Right." I rolled my eyes and returned to my assignment. But before he left, Daniel said something that stayed in my mind for the rest of the day. "You know, you don't have to be perfect for people to like you." I froze. For some reason, those words hurt more than they should have. Because deep down, I was afraid they weren't true. That evening, I returned home and found my parents waiting in the living room. "We have wonderful news," Dad announced. "You've been selected to represent our university in the national academic competition." Mom smiled proudly. "We knew you could do it." I smiled back. "Thank you, Dad." But then he added, "You must win. Our family has a reputation to maintain." My smile slowly faded. I wanted to tell them that I was exhausted. That I wanted one day when I didn't have to prove anything to anyone. Instead, I said the words they wanted to hear. "I won't disappoint you." Later that night, I sat alone in my bedroom and opened my diary. *Dear Diary,* *Everyone thinks I'm perfect. Maybe I've become so good at pretending that even I sometimes forget who I really am.* *I'm scared that if I make one mistake, everyone will stop loving me.* *What if the real me isn't enough?* A tear fell onto the page. For the first time that day, I stopped smiling. Then my phone vibrated. A message from an unknown number appeared on the screen. **Unknown:** *I know you're not as perfect as everyone thinks.* My heart raced. Another message followed. Unknown: And I know what you've been hiding. I stared at the screen, my hands trembling. Who was this person? And what secret could destroy the perfect life I had worked so hard to maintain? I looked at myself in the mirror. For once, I couldn't force a smile. Because someone had finally seen through my mask. To be continued… 💗 End of Episode 1
