OVERDRIVE Episode 1: The Boy Who Never Loses The sound of engines filled the midnight air. Crowds gathered around the underground racing track, cheering as two cars lined up at the starting line. Bright lights reflected off their polished bodies, and the atmosphere was filled with excitement. Everyone was waiting for one person. Adrian Black. The undefeated racer. "He's here!" someone shouted. A black sports car rolled into position. Behind the wheel sat Adrian, a twenty-one-year-old college student known for his cold attitude, fearless driving, and mysterious past. He never lost a race. And he never let anyone get close to him. "Three… two… one… GO!" The cars sped forward. Within seconds, Adrian took the lead. He handled every turn with confidence, leaving the other racers struggling to catch up. When he crossed the finish line, the crowd erupted. Another victory. Another night without a challenge. At least, that was what he thought. The next morning, Adrian arrived at Silvercrest University looking as uninterested as ever. Students whispered as he passed, but he ignored them. He was almost inside the building when someone suddenly stepped in front of him. "Hey! You nearly hit me with your car last night!" Adrian stopped. Standing before him was a girl with long brown hair, an angry expression, and a notebook pressed against her chest. "I'm sorry, do I know you?" he asked. The girl narrowed her eyes. "You were the idiot driving that black car near the campus road!" Adrian raised an eyebrow. "First, I wasn't driving near the campus road. Second, watch your language." "Second? You almost ruined my evening!" "Then maybe you shouldn't stand in the middle of the road." The girl stared at him in disbelief. "You're unbelievable!" "And you're loud." She opened her mouth to argue, but the university bell rang. "What's your name, anyway?" Adrian asked. "Why would I tell you?" He gave a faint smile. "Fine. I'll find out myself." She watched him walk away. "Arrogant rich boy," she muttered. Her name was Elena Cruz. Unlike Adrian, Elena had no interest in expensive cars, racing competitions, or wealthy boys who thought they owned the world. She was a hardworking student who wanted to finish college and help her family. But fate had other plans. During their afternoon class, their professor announced a new group project. "Adrian Black and Elena Cruz, you'll be working together." Elena's jaw dropped. "Sir, can I change partners?" "No," the professor answered firmly. Across the room, Adrian leaned back in his chair, looking amused. "Looks like we're going to spend a lot of time together, Elena." She glared at him. "Don't get your hopes up." He chuckled. "I wouldn't dream of it." Neither of them knew that their rivalry was about to become much more complicated. That afternoon, Elena found a black envelope inside her locker. There was no name on it, only a short message. **STAY AWAY FROM ADRIAN BLACK.** Her smile disappeared. Who sent it? And why was someone warning her about the boy she had just met? Before she could think of an answer, another message appeared on her phone. **Unknown Number:** *If you want to know what happened three years ago, meet me at the old garage tonight.* Elena stared at the screen. Three years ago. The same year her older brother disappeared. Her hands trembled as she read the message again. Suddenly, Adrian's words from that morning came back to her. *I'll find out myself.
