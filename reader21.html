Episode 1: The Secret Behind Her Eyes Genre: Mystery • Drama • Thriller • Supernatural • Suspense Chapter 1: The Girl with the Missing Memories Mia Anderson had one problem that followed her everywhere. She couldn't remember anything about the night her older sister disappeared. It had been five years since her sister, Sophia, vanished without a trace. The police searched their neighborhood, questioned their relatives, and investigated every possible lead. Yet nobody ever discovered what happened to her. Mia was the last person who had seen Sophia alive. But she couldn't remember their final conversation. Every time she tried, she experienced the same nightmare: a dark hallway, a broken mirror, and a voice whispering her name. “Mia… don't trust them.” She always woke up before she could see the speaker's face. At seventeen, Mia had learned to live with the unanswered questions. She attended school, spent time with her best friend, Chloe, and tried to keep her family from falling apart. But everything changed when a mysterious envelope arrived at her house. There was no sender's name. Inside was a photograph of Sophia standing outside an abandoned building. On the back, someone had written: SHE DIDN'T DISAPPEAR. SHE WAS HIDING FROM YOU. Mia's hands began to tremble. “What does this mean?” she whispered. Then she noticed something in the photograph. Sophia wasn't alone. A second person stood behind her, their face carefully scratched out. And around that person's wrist was a bracelet identical to the one Mia wore every day.
