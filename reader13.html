DARK MOON: THE BLOOD ALTAR Episode 1: The Girl with the Crimson Eyes The night the moon turned red, everything changed. Evelyn Rose had always believed that monsters existed only in stories. She never believed in vampires, werewolves, or the strange legends whispered among students at night. Until she met seven boys who seemed to know more about her than she knew about herself. And until the night someone tried to kill her. --- ### The New Girl "Are you sure this is the right school?" Evelyn stared at the enormous gates of Decelis Academy. The building looked more like an ancient castle than a school. Tall towers rose above the trees, and the windows reflected the pale moonlight. "It's definitely the right place," her best friend, Clara, replied. "Come on! We're going to be late." Evelyn adjusted her backpack and followed her inside. Students filled the courtyard, talking and laughing. Everything seemed normal until she noticed a group standing near the entrance. Seven boys. They wore the same black uniforms, but each had a different presence. Some looked friendly, others distant, and one seemed completely uninterested in the world around him. Yet all seven turned their heads at the exact same moment. They were staring at Evelyn. She slowed her steps. "Clara," she whispered. "Why are they looking at me?" Clara followed her gaze. "Oh, those guys? They're the most mysterious students in the academy. Nobody really knows much about them." Evelyn tried to ignore the strange feeling in her chest. But one of the boys stepped forward. He had dark hair and sharp eyes that seemed almost silver beneath the morning light. "What's your name?" he asked. "Evelyn. Why?" He stared at her for a long moment. Then his expression changed. "That's impossible." Before she could ask what he meant, another boy grabbed his shoulder. "Jihan. Stop." The boy named Jihan looked away, but not before Evelyn noticed something unusual. His eyes had briefly turned crimson. --- ### The Strange Encounter That evening, Evelyn stayed late in the library to finish her assignments. The academy was unusually quiet. She packed her books and headed toward the main entrance, but the lights suddenly flickered. Once. Twice. Then everything went dark. "Hello?" A noise came from the hallway behind her. Evelyn turned. Someone was standing at the end of the corridor. She couldn't see the person's face, but she could feel that she was being watched. "Who's there?" The figure moved closer. Evelyn stepped backward. Then a hand suddenly caught her wrist. She gasped and tried to pull away. "Don't move," a familiar voice whispered. It was Jihan. He stood between her and the mysterious figure, his expression colder than she had ever seen. "Close your eyes," he ordered. "What? Why?" "Just trust me." A low growl echoed through the hallway. Evelyn froze. That wasn't a human sound. When she opened her eyes again, the mysterious figure had disappeared. Jihan released her wrist and looked at her carefully. "Did it touch you?" She shook her head. "What was that thing?" He didn't answer. Instead, he looked toward the dark window. "They've found you." "Who has?" Jihan turned back to her. "The ones who have been searching for your blood." Evelyn stared at him, her heart pounding. "My blood? What are you talking about?" Before he could explain, six more boys appeared at the end of the corridor. Their expressions were serious. One of them approached Evelyn and studied her face. "She really looks like her," he murmured. Another shook his head. "We shouldn't tell her yet." Jihan stepped in front of Evelyn. "She deserves to know." --- ### The Blood Altar Later that night, Evelyn was taken to an abandoned chapel hidden deep inside the academy grounds. At its center stood an ancient stone altar covered in strange symbols. The moment she stepped inside, the silver pendant around her neck began to glow. "What is this place?" she whispered. Jihan stood beside her. "The Blood Altar." Evelyn touched her pendant. "My mother gave this to me before she disappeared." The seven boys exchanged glances. One of them slowly approached the altar. "Then there's no mistake." He looked directly at Evelyn. "Your mother was the last guardian of the Blood Altar. Her disappearance changed the balance between our two worlds." Evelyn shook her head. "No. My mother was an ordinary woman." "That's what you were told," Jihan replied quietly. Suddenly, the symbols on the altar began to glow red. A strange voice filled the chapel. **"The bloodline has returned."** Evelyn stumbled backward. The pendant burned against her skin, and a vision flashed through her mind: a red moon, a ruined chapel, and a woman reaching toward her through the darkness. Then she heard the woman's voice. **"Find the truth before the next blood moon."** The vision disappeared. Evelyn fell to her knees, struggling to breathe. Jihan caught her before she hit the floor. His eyes had turned crimson again. "Listen to me, Evelyn," he whispered. "From this moment on, you must never go anywhere alone." She looked up at him. "Why?" He hesitated. Then he gave her an answer that made her blood run cold. "Because the people hunting you aren't the only ones who want your power." He glanced toward the chapel doors. "And one of us may be hiding the truth." Outside, the moon slowly turned red. TO BE CONTINUED… 🌙🩸 End of Episode 1
