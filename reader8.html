I WAS JUST DOING MY JOB Episode 1: My Boss Is a Nightmare “YOU'RE FIRED!” I froze in the middle of the office. Everyone turned to look at me. I blinked twice, wondering if I had heard correctly. “Excuse me, sir?” My boss, Adrian Villareal, slammed a folder onto his desk. “You heard me, Miss Santos. You're fired.” I stared at him in disbelief. “Sir, I only submitted the report you asked for!” “And you submitted the wrong file!” I looked at the folder in his hand, then at my laptop. Wait. Wrong file? My eyes widened. “Oh no…” I had accidentally sent him the unfinished draft instead of the final report. But honestly, was that enough to fire someone? “Sir, I can fix it! Just give me ten minutes.” Adrian crossed his arms. “You have five.” “Five minutes?!” “Four, if you keep arguing.” I immediately rushed back to my desk. That man was unbelievable. Adrian Villareal was the youngest CEO in the company. He was handsome, rich, intelligent, and completely terrifying. Employees called him *The Ice King* because he rarely smiled and never tolerated mistakes. Unfortunately, I was his personal assistant. My name is Clara Santos, and I had been working for him for only two weeks. Two weeks. And somehow, I had already become his least favorite employee. I opened my laptop and corrected the report as quickly as possible. My fingers flew across the keyboard while I silently prayed that my computer wouldn't freeze. “Three minutes,” Adrian called from his office. “I CAN HEAR YOU, SIR!” The entire office went silent. I covered my mouth. Oh, no. I had just shouted at the CEO. Adrian slowly stepped out of his office. “Excuse me?” I stood up immediately. “I mean, yes, sir! I'm working on it, sir!” He stared at me for a moment before returning to his office. I exhaled in relief. Finally, I finished the report and sent it to him. A few seconds later, my phone rang. “Come to my office,” he said. I entered his office nervously. Adrian examined the report on his screen. “It's correct.” I smiled. “Thank you, sir!” “But you made another mistake.” My smile disappeared. “What mistake?” “You forgot to attach the supporting document.” I closed my eyes. “I'm going to lose my mind.” “Did you say something?” “No, sir! I said you're very kind, sir!” He raised an eyebrow. “Get out, Clara.” I hurried toward the door. “And Clara?” I turned around. “Tomorrow, you're coming with me to a business meeting.” I frowned. “Why me?” “Because you're my assistant.” “Right. I forgot that being your assistant means losing my peace.” “What was that?” “Nothing, sir!” I practically ran out of his office. I was going to need a vacation. Or a new job. Preferably both. --- ### The Unexpected Encounter That evening, I stayed late to finish my remaining tasks. The office was nearly empty when I finally packed my things. As I walked toward the elevator, I heard a loud crash coming from the conference room. I hurried over. “Hello? Is someone there?” The door was slightly open. Inside, Adrian was sitting on the floor, holding his arm. A broken glass lay nearby. “Sir!” I rushed toward him. “What happened?” “Nothing. I slipped.” I stared at him. The same man who could intimidate an entire boardroom was sitting on the floor looking genuinely uncomfortable. For the first time, he didn't seem like the terrifying CEO everyone talked about. He looked human. “Don't move,” I said. I grabbed the first-aid kit from the cabinet and carefully helped him clean the small cut on his arm. Adrian watched me silently. “Why are you helping me?” he asked. I looked up, confused. “Because you're injured?” “You spent the entire day complaining about me.” I sighed. “Sir, I complain about you because you give me a headache. That doesn't mean I'll leave you injured on the floor.” For a moment, he said nothing. Then the corner of his mouth lifted. He was smiling. Actually smiling. I nearly dropped the bandage. “Did you just smile?” His expression immediately returned to normal. “No.” “You did!” “Miss Santos, do you want to keep your job?” I quickly finished wrapping his arm. “No, sir. I want to keep my mouth shut.” “Good decision.” But as I stood up, he gently caught my wrist. “Thank you, Clara.” I stared at him. His voice was quieter this time, without the usual coldness. “You're welcome, sir,” I replied. For a moment, neither of us moved. Then my phone rang, breaking the silence. I stepped away and answered it. “Hello?” A man's voice came through the phone. “Clara, don't go home tonight.” My blood ran cold. “Who is this?” Before he could answer, the call ended. I stared at my phone, confused and frightened. “What's wrong?” Adrian asked. I shook my head. “Nothing, sir. Probably just a wrong number.” But when I looked toward the conference room door, I noticed something on the floor. A white envelope. My name was written across it. I slowly picked it up and opened it. Inside was a photograph of me entering the office that morning. Beneath it was a short message. **STOP INVESTIGATING WHAT HAPPENED FIVE YEARS AGO.** My hands began to tremble. I had never told anyone about that night. Not even my closest friends. And somehow, someone knew. “Clara?” I quickly hid the photograph. Adrian was watching me carefully. “Who was that on the phone?” I forced a smile. “Just a wrong number.” He looked at me for a long moment. Then he said something that made my heart stop. “Clara, if someone is threatening you, tell me.” I stared at him, unable to speak. Because suddenly, I realized something. My boss might be difficult, arrogant, and impossible to please. But he might also be the only person who could help me uncover the truth. And I had a feeling that tomorrow's business meeting would change everything. **To be continued…** 🖤 End of Episode 1
