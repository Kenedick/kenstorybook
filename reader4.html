Letters to My Crush Episode 1: The Letter He Never Read Dear Crush, I don't know when I started liking you. Maybe it was when you smiled at me in the hallway, or when you helped me pick up my books without even knowing my name. Or maybe it was the way you laughed with your friends, making an ordinary day feel special. Whatever the reason, there's one thing I know for sure. I like you. And you don't even know I exist. — Your Secret Admirer I folded the letter carefully and placed it inside a pink envelope. Then I hid it inside my notebook before my best friend, Mia, could see it. "Are you writing another letter for him?" Mia asked, raising an eyebrow. "No!" I answered too quickly. She laughed. "Girl, you're blushing!" "Can you please stop?" "Fine. But one day, you're going to give that letter to him." I shook my head. "Never. What if he laughs at me?" "Then he's not worth your feelings." I smiled, but deep inside, I knew I wasn't brave enough to confess. His name was Ethan Reyes. He was one of the most popular students in our college. He was smart, friendly, and always surrounded by people. Meanwhile, I was just an ordinary girl who preferred books, quiet places, and writing down feelings I couldn't say aloud. Every morning, I saw him near the campus garden. Every afternoon, I secretly hoped he would notice me. And every night, I wrote him another letter. He became the reason I looked forward to going to school, even when I had difficult classes or assignments waiting for me. But I never expected that one small mistake would change everything. That Friday, I was rushing to my next class when my notebook slipped from my bag. I didn't notice that one of my letters had fallen onto the floor. Someone picked it up. "Dear Crush?" I froze. That voice. I slowly turned around. Ethan was standing behind me, holding my pink envelope. My heart pounded. "Give it back!" I said, reaching for it. He lifted the letter just out of my reach. "Relax. I haven't read it." "Then why are you holding it?" "It has your name on the back." I blinked. "What?" He turned the envelope around and smiled. "Well, not your full name. Just the letter 'A.'" I quickly grabbed it from his hand and held it against my chest. "Please forget you saw that." Ethan studied me for a moment. "Can I ask you something?" I swallowed nervously. "What?" "Is this letter for someone in our school?" I couldn't answer. He smiled gently. "Don't worry. I won't tell anyone." Then he walked away. I stood there, wishing the ground would swallow me whole. But as I opened my notebook later that afternoon, I found a small piece of paper tucked between the pages. It wasn't mine. The handwriting was unfamiliar, and the message was short. **"If you're brave enough to write a letter to your crush, maybe you're brave enough to let him know how you feel."** At the bottom was a name. **— Ethan** I stared at the paper, my cheeks growing warm. Did he write this? And if he did, why? For the first time, I wondered whether my secret crush might not be so one-sided after all. To be continued. 💌 End of Episode 1
