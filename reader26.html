Chapter 1: Dr. Sheppard at the Breakfast TableSummarySetting the Scene: The novel opens in the quiet fictional village of King's Abbot. Dr. James Sheppard, the town doctor and narrator, returns home from confirming the death of a local widow, Mrs. Ferrars. Village Gossip: Over breakfast, Dr. Sheppard discusses the incident with his inquisitive sister, Caroline, who excels at acquiring local gossip without leaving the house. Circumstances & Rumors: Caroline believes Mrs. Ferrars committed suicide due to guilt over poisoning her late husband a year earlier—a rumor Dr. Sheppard has consistently tried to dismiss. Dr. Sheppard reveals that Mrs. Ferrars died from an overdose of Veronal, a sleeping medication.Connection to Roger Ackroyd: The conversation touches upon Roger Ackroyd, the prominent local wealthy widower. It was widely expected in the village that he and Mrs. Ferrars would eventually marry, leaving the town now suspended in tragedy and speculation.
