Episode 1: Waking Up as His Unwanted Wife “Where am I?” I slowly opened my eyes and found myself lying in a luxurious bedroom. The curtains were made of silk, the furniture looked expensive, and a huge chandelier hung above me. This definitely wasn't my room. I sat up quickly and looked around. “What happened to me?” Suddenly, a sharp headache struck me. Strange memories began flooding into my mind. A beautiful woman. A wealthy family. A cold husband. And a marriage filled with hatred. My eyes widened. “No way…” I remembered the novel I had read before falling asleep. *The Billionaire's Unwanted Wife.* In the story, the main character was named Isabella Montgomery, a woman who married billionaire Alexander Blackwood. Unfortunately, Alexander loved another woman and treated Isabella like a stranger. Eventually, Isabella lost everything. And now… I looked at my reflection in the mirror. The beautiful woman staring back at me was Isabella herself. “WAIT! I'M INSIDE THE NOVEL?” Before I could process what was happening, the bedroom door opened. A tall man wearing a black suit walked inside. His expression was cold, and his eyes showed no warmth. Alexander Blackwood. My husband. He glanced at me before loosening his tie. “So, you're finally awake.” I swallowed nervously. “Alexander?” “What?” he asked impatiently. “Are you really my husband?” He frowned. “Have you lost your memory?” *Technically, yes. But I can't exactly tell you that I transmigrated into your wife's body.* I forced a smile. “Maybe a little.” Alexander walked toward the desk and picked up a document. “Listen carefully, Isabella. Our marriage exists only because of our families. Don't expect me to love you.” His words stung, even though I knew they were coming. In the original novel, Isabella would cry and beg him to stay. But I wasn't the original Isabella. I had no intention of chasing a man who didn't want me. “Don't worry,” I replied calmly. “I'm not asking you to love me.” Alexander paused. For the first time, his cold expression changed slightly. “What did you say?” “You heard me. You can live your life, and I'll live mine. We don't have to pretend to be in love.” He studied me as if I were a completely different person. “What's gotten into you?” I shrugged. “Maybe I've finally learned my worth.” Silence filled the room. Alexander stared at me for a long moment before turning toward the door. “Don't regret those words.” “I won't.” The door closed behind him. I immediately fell back onto the bed and covered my face with a pillow. “Oh my gosh! I'm married to a billionaire, but he hates me!” I sat up again, remembering the tragic ending of the novel. If I followed the original storyline, Isabella would eventually lose her marriage, her reputation, and her future. But not this time. I grabbed a notebook from the bedside table and wrote three rules. **RULE NUMBER ONE:** Never beg Alexander for love. **RULE NUMBER TWO:** Protect myself and build my own future. **RULE NUMBER THREE:** Avoid the original story's tragic ending at all costs. I smiled proudly at my list. “Easy enough.” Just then, my phone vibrated. A message appeared on the screen from an unknown number. **Unknown:** *Stay away from Alexander. You don't know what you're dealing with.* My smile disappeared. Who sent that message? And why did it sound like someone knew exactly what was going to happen? I looked toward the closed bedroom door. Maybe being a billionaire's wife wasn't going to be as easy as I thought.
