ENTANGLED WITH THE FAE LORD Episode 1: The Mysterious Fae Lord Amara Vale never believed in magic—until she met Lucien Thorne, the mysterious Lord of the Fae. One night, she accidentally entered his enchanted forest and discovered a strange silver mark on her wrist. “You don't belong here, human,” Lucien warned. “Then help me leave!” Amara demanded. His golden eyes darkened. “I can't. You've already been claimed by the Fae Court.” Before Amara could ask another question, Lucien revealed a secret that changed everything. “I knew your mother, Amara. And I'm the last person who saw her alive.” To be continued…
