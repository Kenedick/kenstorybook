The Boy I Never Expected to Meet The first day of college was supposed to be simple for me. Wake up early, find my classroom, meet new friends, and survive the first day without embarrassing myself. Easy, right? Well, that was what I thought. “Mom! I'm leaving!” I shouted while fixing my bag. “Don't forget to eat your breakfast!” Mom called from the kitchen. “I'll buy something at school!” I hurried outside, afraid that I would be late on my very first day. When I finally arrived at the university, I was amazed by how big the campus was. Students were everywhere, laughing, talking, and taking pictures with their friends. Meanwhile, I was standing in the middle of the hallway, completely lost. “Excuse me, do you know where Room 204 is?” I asked a girl passing by. “Second floor, turn left!” “Thank you!” I quickly climbed the stairs, but as I turned the corner, someone suddenly bumped into me. My books fell to the floor. “Oh my gosh! I'm sorry!” I said, immediately bending down to pick them up. “It's okay. You should watch where you're going.” I stopped. *Seriously?* I looked up and saw a tall guy wearing a black jacket. He had messy hair, a cold expression, and the kind of face that made people look twice. He picked up one of my notebooks and handed it to me. “Here.” “Thanks,” I replied, trying to hide my annoyance. He nodded and walked away like nothing had happened. “Wow. What a nice way to start the day,” I muttered sarcastically. I continued to my classroom, hoping I wouldn't encounter him again. Unfortunately, the universe seemed to have other plans. When I entered Room 204, almost every seat was occupied. I spotted an empty chair near the window and hurried toward it. But just as I was about to sit down, someone placed a bag on the chair. “Sorry, this seat is taken.” I looked at the person. It was him. The guy from the hallway. “You've got to be kidding me,” I whispered. He raised an eyebrow. “Did you say something?” “Nope. Nothing at all.” I moved to the next seat, promising myself that I would ignore him for the rest of the semester. A few minutes later, our professor entered the room. “Good morning, everyone! Before we begin, I want you to meet your classmates. For our first activity, you'll be working with a partner for the entire week.” The class started cheering. “Please pair up with the person sitting beside you.” I slowly turned my head. And there he was, sitting right next to me. He looked at me and gave a small, amused smile. “Looks like we're partners.” I sighed. “Please don't make this difficult.” “I'm not the one who keeps talking to herself.” “Excuse me?” He laughed quietly. For some reason, that little laugh annoyed me even more. “By the way, I'm Adrian,” he said, holding out his hand. I stared at it for a moment before shaking it. “I'm Alex.” “Nice to meet you, Alex.” “Can't say the same.” He laughed again. And that was the moment I realized something. My college life was not going to be as peaceful as I had planned. Because somehow, out of all the people in the university, I ended up being partners with the most irritating guy I had ever met. And I had no idea that this annoying stranger would soon become someone I could never imagine losing.
