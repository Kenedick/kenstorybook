NO SECOND CHANCES Episode 1: The Day She Lost Everything “Sign the papers, Elena.” Those three words destroyed the last bit of hope I had left. I stared at the man standing in front of me—the same man I had loved for five years, the same man I had sacrificed everything for. Adrian Blackwood. My husband. Or at least, he had been. “You're really doing this?” I whispered, holding back my tears. Adrian didn't look at me. “I've made my decision.” I laughed bitterly. “Your decision? What about everything we've been through? What about the promises you made to me?” He finally met my eyes. “People change, Elena.” I wanted to scream. I wanted to ask him how he could throw away five years of marriage as if they meant nothing. But then I saw her. Standing behind him was Vanessa, the woman he had repeatedly called his business partner. She was wearing my favorite perfume. My heart sank. “How long?” I asked. Neither of them answered. That silence told me everything. I looked at Adrian one last time. “You could have just told me the truth.” He placed the divorce papers on the table. “I'm sorry.” No. He wasn't sorry. He was simply tired of pretending. With trembling hands, I picked up the pen and signed my name. Elena Rose Blackwood. The name I had carried proudly for years was about to become a memory. “There,” I said, pushing the papers toward him. “You're free.” Adrian picked them up without another word. As he walked toward the door, I called his name. “Adrian.” He stopped but didn't turn around. “One day, you'll remember everything I did for you.” He remained silent. “And when that day comes, I hope you understand that you lost someone who would have stood beside you through anything.” He left. The door closed. And for the first time in five years, I was completely alone. --- ### Three Years Later “Miss Rose, your car is ready.” I closed my laptop and stood up. “Thank you.” The woman who walked out of the office was no longer the broken wife who had signed divorce papers in tears. My name was now Elena Rose. I had rebuilt my life from the ground up. I had worked hard, studied harder, and built a successful business without Adrian's money or connections. I no longer waited for someone's approval. I no longer begged anyone to stay. And I certainly never forgot what happened. My assistant hurried beside me. “Miss Rose, there's one more thing. Blackwood International has requested a meeting with us.” I stopped walking. Blackwood International. Adrian's company. I stared at the invitation. After three years, fate had finally brought his name back into my life. “Cancel it,” my assistant suggested carefully. “We can find another business partner.” I looked at the invitation for another moment. Then I smiled. “No. Accept the meeting.” “Are you sure?” “Absolutely.” I slipped the invitation into my bag. “After all, it's just business.” But deep inside, I knew this meeting would be anything but ordinary. Because tomorrow, Adrian Blackwood would meet the woman he once abandoned. And he wouldn't recognize the person standing in front of him. To be continued… 🖤 End of Episode 1
