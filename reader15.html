# THE GREATEST ESTATE DEVELOPER ## Episode 1: The Engineer Who Woke Up as a Noble **Genre:** Fantasy • Comedy • Adventure • Isekai ### A Life-Changing Awakening Kim Suho was an ordinary civil engineering student who loved designing buildings and studying construction. One night, after spending hours reading a fantasy novel, he fell asleep at his desk. When he opened his eyes, however, he was no longer in his room. He was lying in an unfamiliar bed inside a luxurious mansion. “Where am I?” he muttered, looking around the strange room. Before he could get up, a maid entered and bowed. “Master Lloyd, you're finally awake!” Suho froze. “Lloyd? Who's Lloyd?” The maid stared at him as if he had lost his mind. “Master, are you feeling unwell? You are Lloyd Frontera, the young master of this estate!” Suho rushed to a nearby mirror. The reflection showed a young nobleman with unfamiliar features. His eyes widened. “No way. Don't tell me…” He remembered the novel he had been reading the previous night. Lloyd Frontera was a minor character from a poor noble family whose estate was drowning in debt. In the original story, his family eventually lost everything. And now, somehow, Suho had become him. “This is a disaster!” he shouted. ### The Estate's Terrible Condition After gathering his thoughts, Lloyd left his room and explored the mansion. He expected a grand estate filled with servants and treasures, but what he discovered was far from impressive. The walls were damaged, the furniture was old, and the servants looked exhausted. When he asked about the family's finances, the steward handed him a thick stack of documents. “Master, these are the estate's current expenses and outstanding debts.” Lloyd flipped through the papers. His expression changed with every page. “Wait. We owe this much money?” “Yes, Master.” “And the farms are barely producing anything?” “Yes, Master.” “And the roads are so damaged that merchants avoid our territory?” The steward lowered his head. “Unfortunately, that is correct.” Lloyd slowly placed the documents on the table. “This isn't a noble estate. It's a financial disaster!” He now understood why the original Lloyd's future was so miserable. The estate had poor infrastructure, inefficient farming, and almost no reliable source of income. But unlike the original owner, Suho possessed knowledge that could change everything. He had studied engineering.
